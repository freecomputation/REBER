REBER

REBER is the Received Emissions Bulletin and Exchange Register: a public journal where listening stations publish their scrubbed records - emitter dossiers, graded catches, and the unexplained file - in one shared format, so that an emission one antenna cannot explain can be checked against what every other antenna heard.

The register is text-first and stays small on purpose. Its total budget is 20 megabytes, evidence included, and the budget is enforced by the scrub verifier every bundle must pass. The register data itself currently occupies 302877 bytes.
